## Why Hype Is More “Valuable” In Crypto Than The Actual Use Case

### Hype vs use case?

There are so many great projects in the crypto space that came to an end just because people lost interest in them.

Do you even remember Substratum, Neo, EOS, Waltonchain, Electroneum, or, say, Antshares?

Potentially, they introduced you to crypto. And possibly by now you have at least doubled your profits on other coins. More than 90% of projects failed. But if you were a smart man and weren’t greedy, you would’ve made some money!

On the other side, there are projects like SHIB and Doge that are still in the top 20. They have very strong communities and most likely you are a part of them too. Have you told your friends that if these coins will go to $1 you will be a millionaire?

We’re not late to the party. People want easy money. Memecoins have the potential for super easy gains. Do you hear about a new buzz coin? Does the coin still have a low market cap? Does the team have a strong marketing team? Is everyone talking about the coin on Twitter and Reddit?

If you didn’t hear about this “new moon” coin from your friend it is a sign that you are still early.

Sadly enough it’s marketing > product. That’s how the world works! People like to get rich quick schemes and listen to celebrities.

- Billionaire tweets move the market
- Some projects work together with sports events.
- Some projects use brand names in their partnership

People like funny stories too!

Ethereum merge is going to happen soon and everything pumps a little (Don’t get me wrong, the merge is not just hype, it is a really big move). Then you see this photo of Vitalik above and the community starts talking about ETH more. Interestingly enough, that’s a reason to buy more ETH for a lot of people.

In the short term, yes — then hype matters a lot. In the long term, the use case will win. That is why investing in projects with good fundamentals and utility and trustworthy developers is so important.
