## What is Wonderland?

Wonderland is a reserve currency protocol available on the Avalanche Network based on the TIME token. It’s a fork of OlympusDao. Each TIME token is backed by a basket of assets (e.g., MIM, TIME-AVAX LP Tokens, etc) in the Wonderland treasury, giving it an intrinsic value.

The goal of Wonderland is to build a policy-controlled currency system. In the long term, TIME will function as a medium-of-exchange currency. In the short term, TIME intends to optimize the system for growth and wealth creation.

## Pros

- There is no holding period. You can stake and unstake anytime.
- Fast Removal of your initial investment. Stake until your investment reaches 2x and then take it out.
- OHM is the OG. Time is the fork of OHM from ETH to Avax. So, if OHM does well then Time will mostly follow.
- The Treasury will be used then to fund projects like venture capital.
- Wonderland transitioned to a DAO. That’s how long-term value will be created.
- The treasury is also used to do some low-risk yield farming.
- $Ice was exploited. Daniele and his team reimbursed everyone out of their own pockets. $ice has relaunched since.
- $time is being audited. But keep in mind that $time is a fork of OHM which was audited three times already.

## Cons

Like with any project, there are some risks that you have to evaluate:

- Credibility. Daniele — created abacadabra.money, people generally trust him a bit more than a project founder that is completely unknown or doesn’t have a track record.
- APY. It might be sky-high right now, but it’s likely will be lower when more people invest…just look at the APY history chart for Olympus DAO.
- Price. Since you are earning rewards in TIME, you need to carefully monitor the price and compare it against the rate at which you are earning staking rewards. Price may drop and you will end up in a negative position
- $Time is starting as a Ponzi scheme to build the treasury.
