## Let's begin.

The order is not relevant.

There are many other coins that I’d like to mention here but let's keep it simple. These coins have great potential, amazing fundamentals and some of them still haven’t rallied as much as others (SOL, AXS, BNB).

My portfolio is a little bit different, but that’s because I started investing all the way back in 2016. But this is what I would invest in right now:

### Ethereum

Ethereum is already a well-known next-generation platform for decentralized applications, but this is only the beginning. It has great potential going forward, which makes it one of the safest investments in 2022.

### Bitcoin

Bitcoin made its name a long time ago but it’s still considered to be one of the safest investments. While it might not return as much as other coins, it will stand bear market very well. Just keep in mind that some people might lose interest in it in a few years because of new projects with overwhelming potential.

### Tezos

This platform had a very long start but managed to keep its name in the crypto storm. Tezos can be upgraded without a danger of a hard fork which Bitcoin and Ethereum have suffered a lot.

### Harmony

Harmony is designed to be the platform for the creation and use of decentralized applications (DApps) by focusing on random state sharding (allows creating blocks in seconds).

### Terra

Terra and its ecosystem are all about financial services. It has established a number of partnerships with payments platforms, particularly in the Asia-Pacific region. The recently released Anchor protocol offers an amazing 20% APY savings account and ~300% APY liquidity pool rewards.

### Thornchain

THORChain is a cross-chain swapping protocol. It will allow you to swap BTC for ETH without having to deal with any centralized entity. This is a game-changer because nothing like this existed until now.

### PolkaDOT

Otherwise known as the “Internet of Blockchains”, this protocol allows coins, tokens, private and public blockchains to be interoperable with each other by creating a seamless link to other blockchains both on the Polkadot network and ecosystems like Ethereum.

### Mina

The world’s lightest blockchain. Users can rely on SNARK dapps which enables authenticating of information without revealing it. With Mina, you won’t need to download the entire blockchain; all you need to do is to verify only a few of the latest blocks. Rumors say that Naval and Coinbase ventures invested in it too.
