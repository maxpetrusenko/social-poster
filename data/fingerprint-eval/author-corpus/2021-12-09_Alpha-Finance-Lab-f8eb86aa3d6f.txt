## Alpha Finance Lab

### Learn how Alpha Finance Lab can make you money.

Source: alphafinance.io

> Alpha Finance Lab is an ecosystem of cross-chain DeFi products, which consists of products that works together to maximize returns while minimizing risks for users.

Alpha Finance Lab was founded by Tascha Punyaneramitdee and launched in July 2020. It was later followed by a public token sale on Binance Launchpad. The native cryptocurrency of the Alpha Finance ecosystem of products and protocols is $ALPHA an ERC-20 and BSC compatible token. At the moment, you can stake $ALPHA at about 43% APY.

Some of the main projects are already launched on the mainnet and have striking results.

### AlphaX

AlphaX allows any DeFi users to take leveraged long or short positions of any asset without needing to hold that asset with help of Strike Token, which is a completely new take on derivatives trading on-chain.

### Alpha Homora V2

Alpha Homora V2 is a leveraged yield farming(it is similar to bank deposits but with higher APY and not passive) and leveraged liquidity providing protocol on Ethereum and Avalanche networks. On Ethereum it can be integrated with many decentralized exchanges. On Avalanche, the product integrates with Trader Joe and Pangolin decentralized exchanges. Lenders can lend many assets to earn high lending interest rates.

### Beta Finance

Beta Finance is a permissionless money market for short sales, borrowing, and lending. It aims to offset crypto volatility and bring market stability.

### PStake

Pstake allows securely staking your assets to earn staking rewards and receive 1:1 pegged stake representative tokens which can be used in DeFi to generate additional yield.

### Alpha Metaverse

Alpha Buy Wall adds NFTs to their collections. And GuildFi seeks to attain interconnectedness of games, NFTs and helps players to maximize rewards across various play-to-earn games.

Alpha Finance Lab joined the Avalanche Rush Program. The Avalanche Foundation and Alpha Finance Lab allocated a total of $6M ($3M in AVAX and $3M in ALPHA) of liquidity mining incentives to Alpha Homora V2. After launching on the Avalanche network the protocol has organically achieved over $270 million in TVL.
