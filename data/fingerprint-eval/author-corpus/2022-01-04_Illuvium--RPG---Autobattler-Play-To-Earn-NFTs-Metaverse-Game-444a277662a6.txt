## Illuvium: RPG & Autobattler Play-To-Earn NFTs Metaverse Game

### Finally, you can earn money (NFTs) while playing something cool!

### Metaverse is here!

Illuvium is a free-to-play open-world RPG game that lets you build a collection of monsters that can be grown in power over time, and players can use them to venture into more dangerous regions or fight other players. Players can earn rewards as they complete challenges, earn and trade rare in-game items.

There will be five in-game classes: fighter, guardian, rogue, psion (mage), empath.

In the beginning, the game will have over 100 creatures in the form of non-fungible tokens (NFT). Each creature( Illuvial) will be able to evolve and connect with one of five elements.

The beta will be launched around March 2022 and the official launch is scheduled for the summer. The game will include story and battle mode.

Winning players will be able to capture the defeated Illuvials, similar to the crypto Pokemon game.

If you like to gamble you can watch the fights and place bets on the outcome.

With high-resolution beautiful graphics lots of players, who like to play quality games will join and it should drive crypto adoption. The mobile game is planned for 2023 and it will not stand even close with Axie Infinity.

ImmutableX (a Layer2 solution for ETH) will help to make instant and free peer-to-peer transactions between players.

Illuvium token (ILV) will enable inside the game economy. You can also stake ILV tokens to increase currency liquidity. The project also set aside 1 million ILV for rewards and esports and 3 million ILV for participants who contribute liquidity (for three years).
