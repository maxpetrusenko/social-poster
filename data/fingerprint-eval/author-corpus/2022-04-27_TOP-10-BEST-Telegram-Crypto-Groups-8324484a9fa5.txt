## TOP 10 BEST Telegram Crypto Groups

### Be ahead of the game with these Telegram groups!

These Telegram channels are in no particular order. One is better than the other depending on your goal. But in general, I found these telegram groups are most helpful for me personally.

## The TL;DR:

- BitcoinBot
- Altcoins Channel
- Facebook Metaverse Nfts
- Whale Alert
- Airdrop
- ICO Analytics
- CoinBureauInsider
- Lunar Crush
- 100 Eyes Crypto Scanner
- DeFiMillion

### Bitcoin Bot

- https://t.me/BitcoinBot

Place where I get my bitcoin news on a daily basis. Mostly it has posts from Cointelegraph, bitcoin magazine and coin desk

### Altcoins Channel

https://t.me/altcoin

There a ton of altcoin news posted here. Those are reliable sources from big names. Most likely if you will hear your news from here you are already late to the party. Although it is still good to know when things like this happen.

### Metaverse NFTs News

https://t.me/Facebook_Metaverse_Nfts

Metaverse NFTs News is an aggregator that includes lots of multiple NFT sources that are concisely delivered to an end-user.

### Airdrops

### ICO Analytics

https://t.me/ico_analytic

ICO Analytics usually don't include shitcoins and posts are being made right after the official announcement.

### Coin Bureau Insider

https://t.me/cbinsider

Coin Bureau Insider has a large community and has quality information that is gathered from multiple sources and sometimes from the other included channels. If you have a lot of time to read through his long posts you will find a lot of value hidden there.

### Lunar Crush Announcements

https://t.me/lunarcrush

Another note I wanted to add is that there are a lot of signal groups where one should buy or sell. Most of the unpaid ones are scams. Paid ones too sometimes. If you are planning to make quick gains most likely you want to follow the “Sentiment”. It explains how people about feel about a certain coin and whether they want to buy or sell it. I found the Lunar Crush group really helpful to make quick gains. You will just have to sort through shitcoins…

### 100 Eyes Crypto Scanner

https://t.me/CryptoScanner100eyes

This is a bot that detects abnormal price movements and detects what is called “divergence”. It is a great indicator for traders to see that an asset is moving in the opposite direction of a technical indicator. Personally, I always miss those actions, because you always need to be ready for information like this.

### Whale Alert:

https://t.me/whale_alert_io

It has more than 300,000 subscribers. This is the most advanced blockchain tracker and analytics system reporting large and interesting transactions as they happen. It will help you time when to sell/buy coins. If they move money from the exchange to the wallet it's a good indication that they are not going to sell.

### DeFiMillion

https://t.me/defimillion

News, profitable projects, airdrops, everything you need to know in the decentralized finance sphere. Plan to be here if you are a long-term trader and profit maker.
