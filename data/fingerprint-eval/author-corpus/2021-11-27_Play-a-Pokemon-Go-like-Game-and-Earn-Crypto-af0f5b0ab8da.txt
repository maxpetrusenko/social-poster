## Play a Pokemon Go-like Game and Earn Crypto

### Fold launches a new game that rewards players in bitcoin.

Fold is the bitcoin rewards debit card and bitcoin-back shopping app. You can earn bitcoin on everything with the Fold bitcoin cashback debit card. However, I would not recommend their card — too many fees. There are a lot of better alternatives out there like Coinbase or Blockfolio.

Anyway, we are not going to talk about cards today. Fold recently created a game called Fold AR (augmented reality) that will be available within the Fold app. It allows players to explore their surroundings while searching for bitcoin and other rewards.

To create this game, Fold has partnered with Niantic, the company that created Pokemon GO.

The game reminds me of coupon clipping with your phone camera. But imagine how much money you could make if Nintendo actually moves Pokemon Go to the blockchain. This is very similar.

The games haven’t officially launched. There was an update on the App Store for the Fold app and the update notes mentioned they were starting to roll out the game. You can sign up for their email notifications below:
