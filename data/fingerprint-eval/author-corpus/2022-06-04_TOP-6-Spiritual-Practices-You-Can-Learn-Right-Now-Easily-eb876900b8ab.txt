## TOP 6 Spiritual Practices You Can Learn Right Now Easily

I wanted to share with you 5 a few simple practices I do every day that allows me to live a happy and fulfilling life. Before I share this, and before you attempt to try this, please make sure to put your heart and devotion into this. I want you to fully grasp a concept, to live it, to feel it, and embody it with your entire being. Only then can we begin to understand.

### Spending time doing what you like

We all know that this is important, all self-help books tell you about it. But are we actually doing it? We are-but in our own way. We are searching for what we like using excitement. We try so many things but we always come back to a few that are always in our minds. And when we can catch it, this moment to what we’re coming back- magic happens.

So today I will notice things I do and by the end of the day, I will see what I enjoyed and what I didn’t.

For people who already found what they like make sure you engage with this activity at least for 15 minutes every day. Can you imagine a lot of people nowadays don’t know what they like? they just do things because they have to like school, work, or daily chores. You can’t be happy doing things you don’t like. That is that simple!

> When we do things we like we become happier!

Example:Things I enjoy that brings me peace and happiness: reading books, playing the hang drum, talking on spiritual topics, gazing at sunrise and sunsets, traveling to new places, and meeting new cultures.

### Selfless service

Be kind to other people. One act of kindness can go a long way! So many good things will happen to you unknowingly. Just saying something good to another person can make their day and they will pass this energy to others.

Today let’s do something good just because we want to, not because we have to. There is a big difference here. Everything we do from the heart opens another heart and this small spark can bring the fire of love to our world.

Sending love to all of you!

By the way, this idea of one good deed is shown very well in the animated movie “Klaus” or “Encanto”.

### Contemplation

Find just a few minutes either at the end of the day, week, or month to reflect on all the events to see what was planned and what can be done better. Look at what you can improve and what went really well. Make a mental note of each interaction.

This practice will help you to figure out how much energy you invest in different kinds of activities. Now you are one step closer to better controlling your life.

### Exercise

By exercising, I mean any type of exercise whether it’s physical or mental. Yoga, tai chi, or pranayama (breathing exercises) can greatly enhance your body abilities. Meditation, observation, and mental exercises will help you a lot with generating will, energy, and ultimately awareness. Martial arts are usually on both sides of helping us physically and mentally.

So today, pick something you like more. Do 20 push-ups, memorize a poem, find a simple yoga flow, or add a new habit and stick with it for 28 days, then add another one.

If you don’t know where to start but know that you want to improve, just send a comment below and I will help you to find the best exercise that fits your needs!

### Meditation

Meditation is one of the best tools for both beginners and pros.

So today, let’s find a few minutes to sit down, calm down your mind, and observe every thought coming to your mind. Don’t engage with these thoughts, be an observer who knowledges those thoughts and lets them go.

If you don’t know how to meditate or need a guide here is a good meditation for you!

And after meditation don’t forget to smile and appreciate all the things you have in your life 😉

### Awareness

Mindfulness is an art that takes quite a lot of discipline, I started by taking a few minutes a day to just observe because it just felt boring or a waste of time. After being mindful for years now, it’s part of my life, I can’t live without it, I look at my past self and my present self, feeling like a completely different person.

There are enormous benefits to practicing awareness for as long as possible. It’s no wonder that some of the highest performing individuals and successful people practice some kind of meditation or mindfulness. In Tim Ferriss’ bestselling book “Tools of Titans” for example, he discovered that after interviewing 200 high-performance individuals and leaders, more than 80% practiced some kind of spiritual practice. They might not call it this way, but meditation simply helps them to focus on things that matter the most.
