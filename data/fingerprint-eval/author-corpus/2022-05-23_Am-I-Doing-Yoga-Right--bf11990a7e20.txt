## 4 Helpful and Easy Signs To Tell Your Yoga Practice Is Right

### Tips and tricks for doing àsanas.

> There is a power in each move. There is a feeling in each move. There is consciousness in each move.

Some people can say it is pointless to explain what yoga is and how to do it(asana). But hey, let's give it a try.

Do you know that feeling in the morning when you wake up? You feel sore and heavy, but you actually slept through the whole night. Well, this is your body speaks to you — you need some exercise. You do a morning stretch, and in an instant, you feel better!

Do you also know that feeling after a yoga class of lightness, happiness, and easiness? Sometimes it even brings a smile to your face. This is your body saying — “Thank you”.

There is an English word for “try” but most people can understand it as “give it a shot”. Did you hear — “Try Yoga! It will help you.” That’s not what we mean. Don’t do yoga with this in your head.

There is Hindi word “bhakti” that describes what we are “trying” to achieve here. It means — devotion, reverence, and faith. It is very simple actually, when you apply consciousness to anything you do — it becomes beautiful!

## Signs you are doing it right:

### You are able to breathe slowly and deeply while you are executing the pose

When you are stretching you move energies to that part of the body, which makes that part of the body “more alive”. You know that thing that you do even now and don’t even think about it called “breathing”?It makes you alive, isn’t it? So logically, when you consciously breathe into parts of your body they become alive too. How do you do that? Take a yoga pose, and at the point where it becomes uncomfortable just stop and breathe until it's comfortable. Now you’ve sent some “life” there. Congrats!

### You feel light tension executing the pose while focusing on the breath

Stay there, in that tension, and breathe. The longer you breathe the deeper you can go. The more you relax into the pose the easier it will get. If you forget about breathing, that means it’s time to make an adjustment, make an asana easier and return to the breath.

### You don’t question yourself anymore if you are in the pose.

I don’t mean to say you are in a perfect position, there is always a way to improve it and to get better, but you feel it, you know it, you feel the tension, you breathe…

### After completing your practice you are calm and present. Your monkey mind went on a little break.

This message you get from your mind and body after being present in the practice. When you apply bhakti to your practice, you can feel the rewards.

Sometimes is easier said than done, but practice and all is coming!

And don’t forget to smile 😉
