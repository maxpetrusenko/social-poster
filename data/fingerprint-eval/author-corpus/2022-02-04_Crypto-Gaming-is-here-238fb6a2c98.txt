## Crypto Gaming is here

### Or the most fun play to earn games

There are so many games already in the crypto — it is hard to count. There are some fun ones, there are ones you don’t really make any money and there are a few that could be interesting and allow players to earn a few bucks. It is definitely not enough to make living yet unless it is a game with NFTs, but with the rising prices of cryptocurrency, you just need to look into the future.

Let's start with the top games from my list:

### Crypto Royale

Crypto Royale is a fun and addictive game. You can play, bet against matches with other players or buy NFT skins for your character.

You can hit a “jackpot” — it's like 10 USD MAX. Usually, in an hour 1–2$ is really the most you could expect to earn. ROY the token is increasing in value though as it’s being noticed. It has 3 tournaments per day (increased rewards for placement with no buy-in) and an active player community and discord.

We anticipate the launch of RIDE, an in-car entertainment that will be building the first immersive media platform with NFTs, XR games, and play-to-earn on the Elrond network. The game might branch out and your token will be worth even more.

### Gods Unchained

Gods Unchained a unique card game is well-known with the bonus of earning some Crypto and NFTs. You have to earn card packs when you level up and by following certain criteria (rank and win on max games). Some of the cards are sellable, most not (apart from those of the “special packs”). When the forge will be functioning, you will be able to merge together two “common skin” cards two create a sellable one. Once a week you can also earn crypto by claiming from the Blessing of the gods' site

### CoinHunt

Coin Hunt World is a free-to-play/play-to-earn mobile game that lets you earn crypto while exploring your neighborhood. Discover keys and unlock rewards. Play individually or with a team!” (Available in the US, Canada, and UK on Andriod, currently invite-only beta in iOS.)

Players explore their real-world area searching for keys, which are then used to open vaults. When a vault is opened the user is prompted with a trivia question. If answered correctly within the given time limit, the user is rewarded.

Currently, users are rewarded in real Bitcoin or Ethereum, as well as in-game items such as avatar skins, and crafting items. Cubie Blueprints are planned to become NFTs.

### Defi Kingdoms

Defi Kingdoms — It’s not really a game though. It’s just a DEX that they built features around to make it seem like a game. It is built on Harmony (ONE). It’s 2nd to Axie Infinity in terms of volume despite being a couple of months old. Hero NFT’s with a lot of utility, an in-game DEX, and LP opportunity. There’s also a much-anticipated with recently released profession quests.

### Illuvium

Illuvium is a free-to-play open-world RPG game that lets you build a collection of monsters that can be grown in power over time, and players can use them to venture into more dangerous regions or fight other players. Players can earn rewards as they complete challenges, earn and trade rare in-game items.

Axie Infinity

Axie is a new type of game, partially owned and operated by its players. Earn AXS tokens by playing and use them to decide the future of the game! On the downside, it might get boring because there is so much repetition, it is also expensive to start and you have to spend hours to make some money.

### Phantom Galaxies

Phantom Galaxies is a mobile shooter. It is a mech space game. You are a mech that can fight as a spaceship or a mech. Animoca game. There is no token, players had a recent NFT airdrop.

### Sandbox

The Sandbox is a virtual Metaverse where players can play, build, own, and monetize their virtual experiences. They empower artists, creators, and players to build the platform they’ve always envisioned, providing them with the means to unleash their creativity.
