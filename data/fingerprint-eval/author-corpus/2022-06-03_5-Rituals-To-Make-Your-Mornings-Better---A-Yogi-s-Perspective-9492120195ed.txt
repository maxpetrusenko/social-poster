## 5 Rituals To Make Your Mornings Better | A Yogi’s Perspective

Most of your reality is not a given. It is shaped by your expectations, beliefs, and thoughts you have formed about it. A big chunk of these beliefs and expectations are encoded into habits you integrate into your daily life.I’ve learned and adopted habits from some of the world’s most successful people. Each has a unique twist on their morning routine that could save you time, energy, and a lot of peace of mind.

I will explain them daily in more detail. Of course, they won’t work for everybody. My advice is to keep experimenting until you find what works for you.

Personally, I do not set up alarms unless really needed. It is hard to force yourself to wake up early for any reason. That’s why I go to sleep earlier. After multiple tests of waking up late and early I noticed that I’m much more productive in the morning. If I think too much at night time it’s harder to go to sleep because my brain is still on high RPM.

Waking up early helps you develop better habits and improves your day-to-day productivity. You just need to tap the potential of this good habit, which leads to many advantages in both your personal and professional life. ( Tip: fewer people will disturb you in the morning hours because they are all sleeping )

We all know exercise is good for you. But do we actually do it?

I have my daily practice, but it doesn’t include making myself tired or being out of breath. It is called “Ashtanga Yoga”. I wouldn’t recommend starting with it unless you are really goal-oriented. That is just my preference for this moment in life. If we observe, the biggest results we can see from people who are doing it consciously. Do you remember the coach telling you to do two more reps when you can’t? At that point in time, you use so much concentration on your muscles and don’t even think about it. That’s how you must approach all things you do in your life? And I’m not talking about being tired, you can do it without my help, but I’m talking about focus, concentration, and being conscious. Do it for a month, and the results won’t make you wait.

It is no mystery that meditation can have many positive effects on both the body and mind.

Adding a meditation practice to your daily routine can be a life-altering experience.

Can you notice how you feel right after you wake up? Isn’t it beautiful? Nothing worries you until you start thinking about what you need to do today.

Wouldn’t you want to feel peaceful? That’s why I take a little bit of time in the morning right after waking up to meditate, so I can keep in my memory those subtle relaxation feelings and extend them throughout the whole day.

I use warm water, squeeze a slice of lemon and put a little honey and drink this water in the morning after my usual yoga and pranayama practices.

Multivitamins are not a ticket to optimal health. Eating a balanced diet of fresh, whole foods is much more likely to ensure good health over the long term. It’s best if you don’t take a multivitamin to compensate for a poor diet(fast food). But if you are eating healthy, it is good to be aware of some missing vitamins and support yourself this way.

Everything you borrow from the earth has some inertia and we want to keep that at a minimum. I use neem and turmeric on empty stomach to dilate the cellular structure of my body. That allows energy to enter the deepest parts of the body.

You can also generate tremendous amounts of energy in the morning with coffee or nicotine. But those can be very destructive. It is better that one doesn’t simply burst out with energy but is able to hold it and release it by choice.
