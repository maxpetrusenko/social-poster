### What is non-fungible?

Fungibility is the ability of an asset to be exchanged for another of the same kind. A dollar bill can be exchanged for another dollar bill, one share of Tesla stock is worth the same as any other share of Tesla stock. Assets that are not interchangeable, such as your house or “Mona Lisa” painting, are non-fungible.

### What is a token?

A cryptocurrency token is an asset for a specific use, like helping support a network, store value, or make purchases. A token is a cryptocurrency that does not have its own blockchain and lives on another blockchain.

### What is a non-fungible token?

Non-Fungible Tokens (NFT) is a digital asset that represents real-world objects like art, music, in-game items. NFTs are written to the blockchain network to certify the uniqueness of digital goods. They can be bought and sold online:

### NFT use case

- For most people, it is an investment because of the limited value making it a scarcity.
- For some people, it is a way to support their artists.
- Receive royalties. This is the percentage you will earn from NFTs you create every time it is subsequently sold and changes hands.
- For some people, it is bragging rights or access to exclusive places.
- Some people use it for trolling/fun/memes.

But a minted NFT of Mona Lisa might be a legit digital copy, but what if it was posted by a friend of the owner? And the owner doesn’t know about it nor know how to make an NFT. This is the flaw in NFT’s that’s already being abused today. People are stealing/copying art and claiming it as their own to sell it as an NFT. This is how it was before and NFTs do not resolve this issue.
