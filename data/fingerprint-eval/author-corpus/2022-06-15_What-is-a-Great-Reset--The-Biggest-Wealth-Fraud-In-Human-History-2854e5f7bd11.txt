### How about Your future?

As I see the world changing so fast, I want to slow down and take a breath. Our parents thought we need to go to university, get a job, save money, and work more for the future, where did most of them end up? This rat race is never going to end.

We depend on governments to solve our problems so much, that we forget that we can resolve problems ourselves. It becomes too expensive to live. How can life be so expensive?

While these questions come up and worry is maybe it's a great opportunity to rethink the way we live. It will lead us to the path of great awakening. It had already begun. For me, it comes down to these things:

- Get a house with the ability to grow my food
- Install solar panels for electricity
- Have your water source. Re-use water
- Work less because money disappearing fast anyway
- Spend time in meditation to find things that matter
- Become more self-sustainable, and do not depend on the government
