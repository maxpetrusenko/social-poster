## Why You Should Use The Coinbase Debit Card

### Introducing the new way to make money especially in the market crash

Source: Coinbase

### The TL;DR

- 💳 Coinbase Card is a debit card so signing up will not impact your credit score and there are no application fees
- 🚀 Get up to 4% crypto rewards back
- 🤑 Get 1% APY in USDC
- 💰 Get paid in an asset as opposed to credit card points
- 🤖 No fees for having a debit card when using USDC or USD
- 💸 No hidden fees, no card issuance fee, no annual fee
- 📈 Best to use when the market is down to get more worth of crypto

Recently Cryptocurrency exchange Coinbase announced that Coinbase debit cardholders will be able to make retail purchases with their Bitcoin, Ethereum, or Dogecoin holdings by linking the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay. Users will also get a physical card and will have an option to pay with regular dollars (USDC) that can be transferred to a Coinbase account.

Source: Coinbase

Users who will make purchases with their Coinbase debit card can earn up to 4% in crypto-related rewards. 4% back in GRT, XLM, AMP, or RLY and 1% back in BTC, ETH, DOGE, or DAI. Sweet!

There’s a 2.49% conversion fee if you pay with crypto; however, there is no fee if your payment comes from your USDC balance, which you can not only transfer without a fee but also earn while staking.

Also, you get 1% APY when storing your money in USDC.

All you need to apply is to have a Coinbase account. There are no credit checks meaning no hard inquiries! After it’s done, follow these steps:

- Deposit USD on coinbase (no fee).
- Convert USD to USDC (no fee).
- Select USDC as a source for payment for the Coinbase Debit card.
- Select XML as your crypto reward to maximize your gains.

You can use it anywhere Visa’s accepted or use it at an ATM (again, no fees!).

As far as I can tell, there are no hidden fees or anything for this plan aside from potential capital gains on the crypto rewards. Most likely, Coinbase will remove some of the rewards in a long run but for now, this debit card offer is an amazing way to earn some extra money.
