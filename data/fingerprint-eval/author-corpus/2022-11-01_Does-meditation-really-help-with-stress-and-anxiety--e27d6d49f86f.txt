## Does meditation really help with stress and anxiety?

### TL;DR: Yes, it does.

Do you like playing video games all day? Do you feel like not doing anything? Do you become emotionally unavailable often and don’t want to talk to anyone for days? Don’t feel like working out?

### Question:

> I’m 28, Male, usually I’m sedentary, work from home and play video games all day.

> I’m also looking for a way to get rid of stress and anxiety other than the workouts.

> Does it REALLY help as people describe it? is there a possibility it wouldn’t work for me?

### Answer:

Meditation always works. Let's clarify… It is helpful to have someone guide you the first time like an app or a person. Consistency is the only important thing. You won’t feel any changes in your brain of course. If you just do it for 1 minute a day for a week. Just slowly increase the time, don’t force it or rush it or expect anything.

There is nothing to gain. There won’t be anything happening when you meditate with the idea of getting rid of anxiety… You can stop to be anxious right now and create a little moment of peace just take 3 deep breaths. Stretch your chest and force the exhale a tiny bit. The little sense of relief/ the sigh of relief that’s what will cause your body to relax. That’s all. That’s the entire and only thing that’s gonna be the start to handling the anxiety. And then repeat again…

But this might seem very anti-climactic or underwhelming. And it is perhaps if you expect something big or grand. But it’s actually really simple. We as humans will do a lot somehow not to take the easy path right in front of us. We will bend in all kinds of ways thinking the solution is there.

The solution is here has always been here and will always be here. Right now. A relaxed breath. A nice stretch that you can feel. An exhalation with a sigh. Relax your muscles. Be easy on yourself.

Meditation is the most simple thing. There is a reason not everyone is doing it in this world. It’s incredibly counterintuitive to do the simple thing. Because it’s so simple it might seem so hard.

And even if it gets hard don’t forget to smile 😊 .
