## What is Crypto Airdrop?

### No. It's not from Apple. And I won't send you any files.

> Let's say an airdrop is a way to get free money. But because there are many cryptocurrencies listed every day it is hard to keep track of them. If you are careful — one airdrop can be worth your yearly paycheck.

In the crypto world, the term “Airdrop” refers to the public distribution of digital assets. Before it was only tokens, but now it can be NFTs too. Airdrops are a popular marketing technique. By giving away a small portion of the tokens, the company hopes to generate enough attention to promote the project.

### Most important tips about Airdrops

- Never give anyone your private key or seed phrase!
- Never pay for an airdrop!
- Be cautious when providing information about yourself — it can be sold.
- Use separate wallets(non-custodial) without crypto to be safe.
- All airdrops have unique requirements.
- Some Airdrops take months before sending tokens.
