## What is DAO in Crypto?

### Everything you need to know about Decentralized Autonomous Organizations

What is a DAO?

Here are a few good definitions online:

- A Decentralized Autonomous Organization is a type of organization that is managed by computer programs. DAOs automate functions that would’ve otherwise been done by humans, through the use of many smart contracts. DAO distributes risks & rewards among people that share a common economic goal.
- Decentralized Autonomous Organization is an internet-native blockchain-derived investor-directed venture capital fund organization managed by all members. At its core, the objective is to provide a decentralized business model for all future enterprises. Mark Cuban called them “the ultimate combination of capitalism and progressivism.”
- DAO is a risk/reward scheme with a programmable set of incentives that can be distributed between token holders that aim to affect change in the world representing a new(online) type of organization.

### Core features of DAO:

- Clear structure & roadmap — organization vision is set by members, members have full control and decide the future of the DAO.
- Voting — removes the need for political representation and involved parties can participate in important decisions by themselves. Imagine voting for a president over the phone.
- Set of rules — the automated crowdsourced process of decision-making eliminates human error, manipulation, and third parties. The DA organization is represented by transparent computational rules.
- Open source code — secured on the digital ledger across the internet, hardened against forgery by blockchain, and disseminated as a distributed database.
- A funding token — owning a token is what determines your voting power. Usability increases its price.

> Bitcoin in essence is the first fully functional DAO with programmed rules and functional autonomy through consensual protocol; the miners and nodes signal voting through support.

Source: reddit

> Examples of DAOs: Aragon, MakerDAO (MKR), DASH, Curve DAO

### Downsides:

1. Greater control in the hands of those who hold the most coin.

2. We read source code about as often as we read EULAs.

3. No legal framework, yet.

4. New technology and unknown consequences.

5. Upgrading of code is often delegated to experts who understand techno-legal intricacies of code and are therefore a point of centralization.

One may have heard about ‘The DAO hack’, back in 2016.

### On the bright side:

Smart contracts with actual utility, and basically a company that functions without a board room? That really helps illustrate why blockchain’s super important as technological advancement.
