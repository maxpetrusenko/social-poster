## Did You Hear About Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC)

### Why do people buy pixel art?

Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC) is a collection of NFT profile pictures showing apes with randomly generated traits. The collection has around 10,000 NFTs and was launched in April 2021. It is developed by Yuga Labs. Apes are minted on the Ethereum blockchain and individual Bored Apes have sold for a huge amount of money. But what explains the rise of the BAYC?

What is an NFT? As you probably already know an NFT, or non-fungible token, acts as proof of ownership for a digital item, proving that the buyer owns the only version of that item.

Non-Fungible Token (NFT) Explained in Plain EnglishThe hype is real.medium.com

The Bored Ape Yacht Club is an exclusive society mostly owned and promoted by celebrities. It unlocks membership and access to an exclusive Discord server- where owners chat and socialize. Holders also get access to a collaborative digital graffiti board called THE BATHROOM.

Owners can also access other NFT collectibles like Mutant Apes and Bored Ape Kennel Club dogs which can be resold for extremely high prices. Holders of the original BAYC NFTs randomly received an airdrop of a mutant serum vial. The Bored Ape Kennel Club is another collection where wallets containing a Bored Ape could mint a Club Dog NFT. Any secondary sales came with a 2.5% royalty fee for a limited time which was donated to animal charities.

### Why BAYC is so successful?

Some people decided to make BAYC and market it through celebrities(Mark Cuban, Post Malone), then paid other rich people to promote them which lead other rich people to want them so they can seem like they are still at the forefront of cool things. Then after the rich people are posting and talking about bored apes, regular celebrity worshippers also want these NFTS and merch.
