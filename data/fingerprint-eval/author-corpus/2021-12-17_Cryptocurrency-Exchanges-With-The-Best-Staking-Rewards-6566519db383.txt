## Cryptocurrency Exchanges With The Best Staking Rewards

### Grow your money with staking!

> If you’re staking on an exchange then you’re not staking, the exchange is.

Let repeat together: “Not your keys — not your coins”. It means that you don’t truly own your crypto/coins unless you are the only one who can access them, using what is known as public and private keys.

Exchanges will give you a cut of those rewards. The only way you’re actually using the Proof Of Stake algorithm is if you are doing it via an actual wallet. Some exchanges require some minimum for what you can invest. If you are not sure what a Proof of Stake is, I would recommend starting here:

What is Proof of Stake (POS)?Spoiler alert: it is something that will make you money.medium.com
