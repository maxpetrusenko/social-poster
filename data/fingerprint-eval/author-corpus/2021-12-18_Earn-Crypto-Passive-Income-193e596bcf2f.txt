## DEFI Lending:

Source: Omertex Blog

> Allows people to be investors and borrowers at the same time but requires quite a bit of reading about it.

You have the ability to take out a loan on top cryptocurrencies at any time in an entirely permissionless fashion. By using smart contracts, borrowers are able to lock collateral to protect against defaults while seamlessly adding to or closing their loans at any time. Fees are much lower than in the banks and taking a loan on your crypto is not a taxable event.

### Liquidity Mining:

When you provide liquidity to a pool you receive a portion of the fees that are generated by those who are using the pool. These rewards are usually paid to you once you remove your liquidity from the pool.

My choices: Aave, THORChain, AlphaHomora, Synthetix.

### Yield farming

When you provide liquidity to pools like UniSwap (lend you crypto to receive liquidity provider tokens that are basically an IOU representing % of that pool), you can then “Yield Farm” with those tokens by temporarily putting them at the disposal of “project x” and in turn earn more of “project x” tokens. It's pretty much like investing in start-ups. If it succeeds you hold a lot of their tokens, and if it fails you still hold a lot of tokens, but now they are worthless.

Defirate.com includes some of the most popular platforms with the highest reward rates.

## Staking

Exchanges will give you a cut of those rewards, so you do not really have the coin. But the only way you’re actually staking is if you are doing it via an actual wallet.

Staking is a great option to passively earn income through your held coins/tokens and can be done with cryptocurrencies that use Proof of Stake (PoS) protocols.

What is Proof of Stake (POS)?Spoiler alert: it is something that will make you money.medium.com

In most cases, you are locking up your crypto for days/weeks/months. This means staked coin cannot be traded during this lockup period while it is being staked/unstaked.

My top wallets for multi-coin staking: Ledger X, Exodus, Trust Wallet, Atomic Wallet, and Crypto.com DeFi Wallet.
