## Spiritual Perspective: Is Weed Good Or Bad? Can you get Enlightened?

### Can cannabis help you with your spiritual growth?

### To me the answer is pretty straightforward:

- It is good when you use it rarely and with a good intention
- It is bad when you use it recreationally and get addicted to it

Here is a longer explanation…

Plant medicines are literally the oldest form of spiritual practice known. Books like “Supernatural: Meetings with the Ancient Teachers of Mankind” by Graham Hancock show us the proof in a form of stone art in caves across the world that will lead the thoughtful readers to understand that entheogen plants were always used.

Research Shamanism, it was the precursor to all magic we have today. All cultures of the world have it. People might say that drugs have never been historically linked to spiritual practice but that’s simply not true.

Research Eleusinian Mysteries, Soma, Datura, Peyote, San Pedro, and ayahuasca. They are all spiritual.

Traces of weed have been discovered with lost passages of the old testament. There are even scholars who argue parts of the bible were written by people under the influence of some kind of psychedelic “drug”. The drugs we take shape our culture and society.

Everyone does drugs… you go to the pharmacy, you drink coffee, you eat sugar, you drink alcohol, you eat food, you breathe.

### Researches

While I can find bad research about weed, I can find good ones too. It all depends on a perspective.

Sum up of negative research:

- Do not smoke weed.
- Cannabis affects your brain in a bad way
- Brain development stops in the late 20s, but you still can get addicted

Sum up of positive research:

- Helps to grow mentally and spiritually if used as an entheogen
- Having a good intention makes all the difference.

### Interviews

Here we should ask people who used marijuana and do meditation and see what they say.

> When I started smoking and would get really high I would have certain revelations about myself and my life. I think if you use it right there could be a decent spiritual component to it.

> I only speak on my own experiences. Weed can be effective in healing yourself in a myriad of ways. However, I became addicted and weed dampened all of my gifts. I feel 100× more connected to source when I am sober. I use mushrooms medicinally once in a while, but that’s really it.

> If your vibration is lower than the vibration of marijuana, consuming it will elevate your vibration. However, if you’ve “ascended” and are already operating from a higher vibrational frequency, smoking marijuana will lower your vibration.

> I’ve found for me a lower dose can enhance the meditative practice but anything above 5mg or so will make my brain completely useless, fun for a recreational buzz, but not so good for spirituality

The general consensus is that while use seems helpful at first, you eventually realize that u are doing less spiritual work which makes it harder to experience meditative clarity. So weed can be good at the beginning of your journey, but as more experienced you get the less you should use it.

It can remind you of what is important if fall into your daily routine and forget to practice regularly. Live will not become easier, you should become better. The better we become the more balance we have. Balance is the key.
