## Top 5 Reasons Why You’re Not A Crypto Millionaire

### You Will Never Become a Crypto Millionaire

### You invest in shitcoins and listen to random influencers

Are you the one following new shitcoins? are you the one that listens to social media influencers? Then you don’t have a chance.

I’ve been around the game for a while. I remember investing in EOS, Smart Cash, and Electroneum thinking “This is it!”.

Was it? No. And every time the project failed.

### You use trading bots

Then I bought different trading bots and told myself that I was gonna become a day trader, and this is how I was gonna make my millions!

Did I succeed? I lost a lot within a month.

### You sell everything when the price is low

Everything crashed, bitcoin was doomed, and I left the crypto-world sad and betrayed. Bitcoin was never going to succeed, what even is this tech? Who is actually gonna use it?

This was 5 years ago (Bull run of 2017/18). Bitcoin went to around $3000. Everyone thought it was dead.

It wasn’t until I had lost hope, and didn’t check prices that I thought clearly and started to actually take a look in the mirror and take accountability for my mistakes. Once you stop trying to follow the current trends and invest in sound projects you might have a chance.

Don’t be blinded by all the profits, because if you do — you will never have it. You will still be the same emotionally fragile person that you’ve always been. You will not sell when is a bull market and you will not buy the dip. Stop reacting to the slightest change, and take a second to think.

Once you live through a few of the “BTC is dead” cycles you catch the rhythm and understand how it works.

### You don’t do the research

Do your own research, look if the trend already happened before, read the whitepapers, look up the devs, make your own decisions and stop trying to take the easy road by being spoonfed (most likely false) information by someone else.

### You only invest in trending projects in the bull market

The best projects come out successful in the bear markets. They have the means to survive, they have the purpose. Monitor those projects. If the project was created and popularized during the bull market most likely it will fail because everyone is trying to make money.

When you make a mistake and get burned, learn from it. Don’t push it under the rug and hope it doesn't happen again, because it will. Over and over, until you learn your lesson.

Most people don’t want to take the hard road, but if you do, you will get your reward eventually. The slower you grow your fortune, the more stable it will be.
