## What is Proof of Stake (POS)?

### Spoiler alert: it is something that will make you money.

Source: datadriveninvestor

> Staking is a way of earning rewards for holding certain cryptocurrencies.

Staking is a consensus algorithm where a person can validate transactions (support network) according to how many coins they hold. The more coins owned by a miner, the more transactions they validate.

Basically, you can make money and secure the network at the same time while you sleep and there is no need to spend an enormous amount of electricity as Proof-of-Work does.

Staking often requires a lockup period, where your crypto can’t be transferred for some time as it is being used.

However, it’s not all roses and diamonds. Staking is much better than Proof-of-work, but it still has its drawbacks.

Imagine you have 5 ETH and your neighbor has 1000 ETH. Your neighbor will earn more rewards than you. In the long run, that means your neighbor will end up with a much larger profit margin. So a larger stakeholder would grow his ETH stake faster than a smaller stakeholder. After a point, it centralizes the network towards the big stakers, and again, the rich get richer faster.

But hey, you have to start somewhere. Now your money is working for you!

Just keep staking!

## How to start staking?

- Learn about crypto that offers “staking”.
- Buy the coin you want.
- Move it to an exchange or wallet that allows earning through staking.
- Go to the Stake/Earn section and select the coin you bought.
- Click “Stake” or “Delegate”.
- Enjoy your rewards!
