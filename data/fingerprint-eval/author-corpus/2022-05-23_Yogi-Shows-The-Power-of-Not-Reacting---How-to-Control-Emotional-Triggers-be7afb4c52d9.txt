## Yogi Shows The Power of Not Reacting | How to Control Emotional Triggers

### Control your reaction like a pro.

> Keeping calm when you’re being triggered is a superpower.

Did you notice that burning in your body? With increased pressure, you don’t hear anything around you and your breath became much faster, you are furious, and your nervous system is reacting.

Congrats, you are in fight or flight mode!

### An emotional trigger

An emotional trigger refers to the experience of having an emotional reaction to an event when something or someone is not the way we want it. You feel like you are losing it. What happens is you are losing your consciousness and acting unconsciously. You might feel regret afterward. Your feelings are valid, they are your cues that something isn’t right. Hey, don’t be upset, it happens to the best of us!

Your triggers are Your teachers. Our work is to heal it

And don’t believe if your mind tells you if you will have a more peaceful life and surroundings if you would live in the forest. Would it be so much easier for me to awaken spiritually? Maybe, shortly, and it won’t be long lasting as you didn’t resolve it, you just run away.

So those annoying people around you force you to go deeper and those are great opportunities. So when the tensions build up it is an opportunity coming at you. The more conscious you are, the more often you can see an element of choice. You can react or you can allow the situation to be as it is. And here is the good thing about it. You actually noticed it. You are like 30% already there.

If your mind is a garden, and your thoughts are seeds, you can either grow flowers or grow weeds

If you are in flight or fight mode you can do these things, they will help shift your focus from the problem to something else

### Things you can do with your body:

- breath(deep belly breath), an ancient yogic exercise that will move energies
- random fast pace exercise(like 100 pushups) will bring your attention to your body and will change your focus to other important parts of your body
- go for a walk, fresh air helps. it is basically nature doing belly breathing for you and things around you might change your focus and you can forget about your problem.
- Stand on nails(google nail boards). Oh believe me you won’t have time to think about that problem because something else will hurt you even more

### Things you can do with your mind:

- observe your behavior and actions.
- question yourself. Why did I do that? How do I feel about it? In which part of the body do I feel it?
- Repeat the mantra. you can say it yourself or listen to it.

## What really helped me

> Be around people who handle pressure well, so that you can learn from them

I invite you to do this practice with me, if you do this one thing, your life will get better.

Invite presence to your life daily, notice how you feel, notice your surrounding, find something beautiful near you and give it your attention, and enjoy it. Do not label or name things, just be there. No thinking is required, just observation.

The more you do it in regular life the easier it will be in challenging situations. It might not come to you right away. You might not be able to catch it in a moment

But as your presence in you grows, you will start noticing patterns that are not you, and when you separate yourself from the situation more options will arise.

In the beginning, the presence will come in at the end and this is good progress as you can actually catch it.

As you practice presence more (that would be somewhere in the middle) you will start finding moments when you trying to change the pattern, but you don’t succeed. That is great too, you are one step closer!

It might take you quite a bit of time to change the pattern, but don’t give up. The moment anger wants to come in, the awareness is already there. That’s a place where you have to make a choice, once you make a different choice, once you made the right choice, it will get a lot easier!

I invite you to do this practice when you are calm, so it will be easier for you when you are not.

What actually happens is you find parts of yourself that are not you( the ego), they no longer serve you, and they have to be let go. Ego can operate only unconsciously. When you bring light(attention) to it, now it is something you recognize and you see it is not you anymore.

This is a part of the spiritual journey, welcome it and accept it. If it were not part of the awakening process you wouldn't be here reading about it. You would be just unconsciously repeating your old behavior day after day and you would pass it to your family and friends and so on…Until someone like You would notice it. Don’t be guilty about it because the moment you know that you lost it, there is an awareness. And this is amazing, you lost it, but now you know that you lost it.

And don’t forget to smile 😉
