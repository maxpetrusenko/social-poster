## 7 Most Controversial Things About Crypto You Need To Know Today

### Are your crypto investments safe?

### Yes. Cryptocurrency is secure and anonymous

But. blockchains are transparent and everyone can see transactions. Companies like Chainalisis find accounts and connect them to users via KYC

There are blockchains like Monero and algorithms like zk-snarks that store information about their customers (most of them are trying not to mention it though).

### Yes. A lot of people say that cryptocurrency is a way to stay away from banks/government and protect your wealth.

But, exchanges prevent people from making withdrawals. What is the difference then, you ask? You still keep the money and you can choose an exchange that works.

### Yes. You can use cold storage to protect your crypto, just as you can use paper cash to protect your cash.

But you will need to make a transfer and use an exchange or a bank and your crypto or money can be locked out.

No exchange is available right now? Find a local company that would like to exchange crypto for cash.

### Yes. You can earn money on stablecoins.

But look at Luna (Terra). CBDCs will give more power to governments. Governments already have all the power they need to block your financial life. What’s the advantage of crypto or stablecoins here? They are a bridge to the crypto world and it is still a work in progress. Yes, they are not transparent(UDST) and everyone should be cautious about using them.

### Yes. Crypto Enthusiasts say that third-party “bank” is removed to make transactions cheaper.

But. Transactions fees are enormous for Bitcoin and Etherium, sometimes even more expensive than using a traditional bank.

Developers are working towards lowering fees as more people start using crypto. There are also alternatives to these cryptocurrencies if at the moment price of the transaction is high.

### Yes. Bitcoin is a store of value.

But. Nothing with the extreme volatility of Bitcoin, Ethereum, etc can be considered a good store of value. A store of value implies low volatility and an asset that at least keeps up with inflation.

In the short term, yes, but in a long run there will be no bitcoins printed, so it will be one of the best stores of value.

### Yes. The government guarantees up to a certain amount of your cash if the bank goes under.

But. Exchanges can get hacked or go down and there is no one who will return your money. Every crypto investor knows “Not your keys, not your coins”, so please, don’t keep your money on the exchange.
