## Top 5 Powerful Spiritual Courses to Become a Better Self

### Practice is the best way to spirituality.

In this day and age when more and more people are looking for spiritual awakening or spiritual development or just peace. More and more online Spiritual Courses, events, workshops, and retreats are becoming available.

I decided to share online spiritual courses that I have personally followed over the last few years. I can recommend them from my personal experience as I know how hard it is to find and pick the right course.

### Self-Realization Fellowship

“Self-Realization Fellowship is…… an invitation to the spiritual seeker to journey together on a life-transforming discovery of the soul. This journey embraces the “how-to-live” teachings of Paramahansa Yogananda, which embody the highest techniques for realizing who we truly are and show how to bring lasting peace, joy, and love into our lives and into the world.”-yogananda.org

I personally love this course; in fact, I used to practice it daily for a few years.

### Inner Engineering

Sadhguru calls inner engineering a ‘Technology’ and not a religion or belief system. He says repeatedly that this isn’t something to be believed, but something to be experienced. The lessons and practices are designed that way.

The Inner Engineering Online Course involves seven sessions of about 90 minutes each. To put it simply, It’s a self-improvement program from a spiritual point of view.

If you’ve seen Sadhguru on YouTube and he resonates with you I would strongly encourage you to take this course! I took the online version of this course last year and it was an amazing experience. One thing to be aware of — once you watch the lesson, you can’t access it anymore. So you’ll need to be fully present to make the most out of it!

### The Art of Happiness

The art of happiness course will help you find the present moment. As a result, it will boost your energy, make you calmer, and clear your mind. There is both an online and on-campus course. Teachings and practices are great as long as you don’t do follow-up courses!

And as always, make sure to research AOL for yourself before commiting. Reddit and Quora are great resources!

### No Mind

This is the new course by Osho international. A 7-day course of 2 hours a day. You will like a dynamic meditation. Silent sitting can be hard in the beginning but if Osho's teachings resonate with you, then this course is definitely worth your time!

### Esalen Institute

Esalen is located in Big Sur, California. There are multiple workshops a week including communication retreats, Tai Chi, mindfulness, yoga retreats, meditation, spiritual workshops, and more. This is definitely the place for you if you are looking for a beautiful place to visit and learn a lot.

I love spiritual retreats. You always learn something new. If you have never been to a spiritual retreat this is a place to go.
