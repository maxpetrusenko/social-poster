## Must-Have Cryptocurrency Apps to Use in 2022

### Top 10 apps for advanced cryptocurrency users

Let me tell you at the beginning, It won't be Robinhood, CashApp, Webull, or Paypal. If you are using those apps you do not own any crypto — those apps do.

Let's move these apps into categories: wallets, exchanges, VPNs, cryptocurrency price trackers, and lending/borrowing platforms.

### CoinMarketCap

CoinMarketCap is a must-have app on your phone, It is the world’s most-referenced price-tracking website for crypto assets. It has high quality and accurate information about the latest changes in the market, where you can buy certain coins, and where you should start your research if you found a coin you like.

## Cryptocurrency Exchanges

For the full list of exchanges with the best staking rewards follow the link below:

Cryptocurrency Exchanges With The Best Staking RewardsGrow your money with staking!medium.com

Coinbase is a secure online platform for buying, selling, transferring, and storing cryptocurrency. They created an open financial system for the world to help convert cryptocurrency in-and-out FIAT currency. Coinbase also has a Coinbase Pro product for more advanced users to trade crypto with much lower fees.

Crypto.com is a cryptocurrency exchange app based in Singapore. It supports trading, investing, staking, wallets, NFTs, and more.

Binance is a cryptocurrency exchange that is the largest exchange in the world in terms of the daily trading volume of cryptocurrencies. It was founded in 2017 and is registered in the Cayman Islands. It has the most amount of features and coins.

Kucoin is a large cryptocurrency exchange offering the ability to buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies. Users can also choose to stake or lend their crypto to earn rewards. Compared to some competitors, KuCoin offers low trading fees, making it an attractive option.

### Crypto.com vs. Coinbase vs Binance vs Kukoi: Which Should You Choose?

If you are thinking between those or any others exchanges — don’t. Most likely you will end up with all of those on your phone because of the location(be careful if you use VPN)because there might not be the coin you are looking for, maybe because of the fees or KYC.

Cryptocurrency exchanges that are good for newcomers are Coinbase and Crypto.com. Binance has a lot more features and is better for more advanced users. Also, some exchanges might not be available in your country. You can substitute those with Kukoin, Kraken, Gemini, or others.

### Nord VPN

NordVPN is the best VPN if you’re looking for peace of mind when on public Wi-Fi. Securely access personal information or work files, encrypt your internet. It has applications for Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS, and Android TV. Manual setup is available for wireless routers, NAS devices, and other platforms

If you are an advanced crypto user most likely you will use a VPN service. Countries make it really hard to buy/sell the coin you might need. That’s why services like NordVPN or Express VPN become handy.

## Celcius

Celcius Network is a platform to earn, borrow, and pay on the blockchain. Users can earn up to 17% APY on crypto, and get a crypto-backed loan for as low as 1% APR. Similar platforms include names like Nexo or Aave.

## Cryptocurrency Wallets

Exodus is a non-custodial wallet that offers great support and extensive control over crypto assets with a friendly user interface. Allows staking.

Trust Wallet is a crypto wallet. You can send, receive and store Bitcoin and many other cryptocurrencies including NFTs safely and securely. Allows staking.

MetaMask is a software cryptocurrency wallet used to interact with the Ethereum blockchain. It allows users to access their Ethereum wallets ( ETH and ERC-20 tokens) through a browser extension or mobile app, which can then be used to interact with decentralized applications.
