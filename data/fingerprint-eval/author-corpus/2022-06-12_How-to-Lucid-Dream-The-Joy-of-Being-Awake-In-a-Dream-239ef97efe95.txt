## How to Lucid Dream?The Joy of Being Awake In a Dream

### I woke up in a dream in less than a month.

Disclaimer: Lucid Dreamers if you want to add something, please do so in the comments. Let’s help each other have some fantastic dreams!

Have heard about this great thing called lucid dreaming (LD) where people fly around, have sex with their dream companions, and do the craziest things their brains can imagine. You are in the right place. It is not very easy, on average it will take around 1 month (it took me about a month of consistent work), but I know people who achieved it just in 3 days!!!

Let's learn about the most important things first!

Dream recall (very important)

> Dreams are vivid is because you remember them. Vivid dreams are highly dependant on dream recall.

You have to remember your dreams! Period. Write down your dream each time you wake up. The more details the better. Writing down your dreams helps you remember them. The act of recalling your dream or at least trying to remember it increases your chance of a vivid dream. Vivid is your best chance to become lucid. Also, there are a lot of situations during our day that can help bring the memory of a dream.

I’ve made for you a “Dream Journal” in Notion. Just duplicate the template and you are good to go. It will help you track your dreams and your progress. I use it myself. Really helps.

Reality Checks(Very! Very important)

What’s wrong with this picture?

A reality check is an exercise during your waking time to test if you are actually sleeping. It helps to become lucid once you are in a dream. Repeat them at least every hour. The more attention you give the exercise the easier it will be to wake up in the dream. Once you become an advanced LDer you might mix reality and dream, so do not forget about them!

Things that work

- Try to read something or look at the digital screen. Move your head away and read again. If the text doesn't make sense or change — you are in the dream
- Push your finger through an object or palm of your hand. Yes your finger can move through your hand in the dream
- Shut your nose and try to breathe.
- Look around for anything unusual, and pay attention while doing so.
- By doing reality checks you stabilize the dream, more on that further.
- Check your reflection in the mirror to see if it looks normal.

REM PERIOD (very important)

Lucid dreams occur in the REM period, so to lucid dream, you have to wake up right before this period. They are different for everyone so you will have to experiment. Start from longer REM Periods. Set your alarm to 3:33 am 4:44 am and 5:55 am.

> If you wake up very tired, you are in your REM period and it should be really easy to enter a lucid dream.

METHODS (misc but important)

You could’ve heard about MILD, WILD, DILD, SILD, WBTB, and so on. You don’t have to remember all the names, and it doesn’t really matter, You will either enter the dream when you already sleeping(SILD) or while you falling asleep(WILD).

Sleep-induced Lucid Dreaming — you enter a Lucid Dream through a regular dream. SILD dreams are usually very fuzzy and not vivid at all. And on top of it, you have to stabilize a lot.

Wake-induced Lucid Dreaming is when you enter the dream directly and are aware during the process. This makes your dreams 1000 times more vivid and easier to remember. For me personally, it is harder to achieve. It is usually harder for the beginner unless you have a strong intention.

Lucid dreaming is highly based on intention, and this means you just need more of it and more often!

### Visualization ( important, but only if you can do it)

Daydreaming about a place you want to be in your sleep. It has to be very specific, Imagine all the details, smells, feelings, and sounds. Close your eyes and fantasize about it for as long as you can.

So right before you go to bed, visualize your good place as much as you can while you are falling asleep. You might have to stay still for a long time and catch the moment. What can help is repeating as you fall asleep affirmation/mantra — “I will lucid dream tonight!” or “Am I Dreaming?” while doing visualization practice. ( what helps me is to imagine anything moving with my closed eyes. People report that visualizing grey noise on the virtual screen helps to make it a “Dream Portal”)

HOW TO GET A LUCID DREAM TONIGHT

- Set up your alarms.
- Do not use a bright digital screen( phone, computer, tablet, TV)
- Consider how long it takes for you to fall asleep:

For people who fall asleep fast — get up from a bad and do something for a few minutes (maybe use a restroom or do a few pushups).

For people who fall asleep slowly - Set a very calm alarm. Try to keep your eyes closed too. The goal is to turn off your alarm with the LEAST amount of movement possible, so keep your alarm close to your bed. You can find an alarm app that turns it off automatically. Just make sure you can actually wake up

- Get ready to fall back asleep
- The best position is the position you woke up in with the least amount of movements (But more vivid dreams happen when you lay on your back, for me it is harder to fall asleep this way)
- Close your eyes and try not to open them during the next step
- The goal is to relax your body and let your body fall asleep while your mind stays awake. If you are already sleepy you can achieve this really quickly. Imagine yourself falling deeper into your bed after every exhalation.
- Observe what your body feels. You will feel something from the image below. Your body will start to numb and soon you won’t be able to feel some parts. You may even feel that some body parts are in a distorted position when they are actually not. Do NOT check! You are 1/3 of the way, there.
- “It won’t work, let's try tomorrow” phase.

The hard part is here. Your body will send you signals to itch, roll over, swallow, yawn, you will feel very bored, etc… The trick here is not to follow those signals. Your body is testing you before sending you out to the dream world. It might take you a “couple of tries”. If you do anything in this phase, it will be easier just to go to sleep than try again the same night as your body “guarding” will increase a lot. It gets very annoying. Save your time and don’t move! Hope I will see some Lucid dreamers agree with me on this in the comment section. This will mark like 2/3 of the way.

- Entering the dream.

This is where you will enter the dream. What helps me is meditation during the day to increase my willpower at night. Your mind will send you so many different thoughts that will carry you away to the regular dream. The goal is to watch your thoughts and not get very excited( otherwise, you will wake up here). Be neutral, like in meditation. Let all thoughts pass and follow your breath.

Now you are on the dream border (3/3) Your secret key here is intention. You can call your intention “imagine” or “feel”. Now feel/imagine yourself rolling out of bed. Another thing you can try to feel/imagine pulling yourself up the rope. You will feel an interesting satisfying sensation. It might take a couple of tries before you successfully can imagine that. Feel your room around you, feel opening your eyes. Your dream will slowly load and become more vivid. The more you involve your 5 senses the better your dream and the more vivid it gets. You can touch, smell and hear things. I rub my hands or spin around myself.

- Do reality checks and stabilization here every so often. Remember you are dreaming.
