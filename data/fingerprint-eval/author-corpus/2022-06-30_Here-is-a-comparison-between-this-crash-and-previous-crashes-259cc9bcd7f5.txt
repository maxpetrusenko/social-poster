If we look back at bitcoin prices we will see 4 or 5 all-time highs followed by a deep crash. Bitcoin gurus will tell you it’s a cycle. Patterns keep repeating themselves. But in reality, no one knows the future. The idea of “cycles” is pretty overblown. The idea that the rest of the time prices should follow some pattern set by any previous “cycle” is just not how markets work.

Halvings do cause a supply shock on a regular basis. What is ‘the halving’? Bitcoin halving is the process of reducing Bitcoin mining in half after each set of 210,000 blocks is mined. Bitcoin halving limits the supply of new coins, so prices could possibly rise if demand remains strong.
