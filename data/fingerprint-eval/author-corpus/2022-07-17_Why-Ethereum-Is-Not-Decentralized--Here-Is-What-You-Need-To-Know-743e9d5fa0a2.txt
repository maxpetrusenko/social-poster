## Why Ethereum Is Not Decentralized. Here Is What You Need To Know

### And how you can help.

Ethereum gets a lot of love from the crypto community for being properly decentralized, but has anyone actually taken the time to analyze that network using Ethereum’s own topology tools? A Twitter user noticed something quite obvious that maybe none of us have seen before:

“Few will acknowledge that of ETH’s ~5000 mainnet nodes, a full 2/3 are hosted, 50% with AWS, 17% of which is in a single data center in Ashburn, VA. As it was said by venerable eth guy “they are a party to your network.”

So numbers will change daily, but it is really scary what is happening now.

https://ethernodes.org/

One-third of the nodes are running on AWS. Which is a very high number.

> Total number of nodes: 4546

> Number of nodes that are hosted: 3052 (67%)

> Number of nodes that are run at the homes of users: 1374 (30%)

> Number of hosted nodes on AWS: 1500 (33%)

Honestly, if you research this topic more you will find that something like 70% of the world's internet traffic travels through Ashburn, Virginia. It shouldn’t necessarily be concerning to find significance in that area. So much web traffic appears to originate there. It’s like the backbone of the internet with all the data services. Any admin who looks up google analytics for their web pages will recognize Ashburn. But let's get to the solutions.
