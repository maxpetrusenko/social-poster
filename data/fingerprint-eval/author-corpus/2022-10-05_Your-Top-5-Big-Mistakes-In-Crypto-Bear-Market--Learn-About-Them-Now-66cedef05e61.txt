## Your Top 5 Big Mistakes In Crypto Bear Market. Learn About Them Now

### How to Avoid Crypto Bear Market Mistakes and Be Ready For The Next Bull Run

You also can’t lose money in a bear market if you already lost it all. I’ve lost quite a bit on Anchor Protocol and borrowing money in DeFi (the collateral ratio can change fast during crashes). When a project fails there is not much you can do if you don’t act quickly. You might want to believe the creator of the token, but most people care for their own interests. Here, you’ve been warned.

Sell crypto in a crash

Massive crashes can happen anytime and really fast. Most of the time there will be signs of a crash. If something goes up really quick it is bound for the correction

Not having patience

Buying a coin and expecting it to do x100 immediately. Don’t list to “If this coin goes to $1 I will be a millionaire.” After hearing so many stories about Dogecoin and Shiba you may want to expect the coin to do to grow 10 times or 100 times in a few days but after a week or month, it might even go down. You might want to wait a little bit longer

Wasting time staring at charts

If you believe in your investment, then all you need to do is buy and forget. ( this is not related to Doge or Shiba) You can check every few days but there is no need to stare at the charts all the time.

Don’t let your crypto work for you

I’m not talking about DeFi. This might be too dangerous. But if you don’t stake then you are losing money.

Learn about gas prices before you start buying crypto.

If you are going to buy tokens on the Ethereum network you might want to learn what gas fees are. It might be more expensive than the token you are trying to buy.
