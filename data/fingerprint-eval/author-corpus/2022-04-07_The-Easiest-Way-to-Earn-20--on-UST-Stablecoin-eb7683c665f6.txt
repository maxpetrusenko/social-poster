Source: OKCoin

### The TL;DR:

- Download the OKCoin app ( or use their website).
- Pass KYC ( for large amounts over $2,000)
- Deposit USD via ACH (instant)
- Buy UST with USD
- Click Earn
- Select UST (Anchor protocol)
- Enjoy 20% APY

## Lets Begin

Head over to the website or Apple Store(link) or Google Play(link) to download the app

Sign Up if you haven’t already:

For larger sums of money, you will have to pass KYC, you might need to wait a little bit. If you are on the website you should go to the verification page.

If you are using the app, make the spread as low as possible by switching to Pro

Go to My Assets -> Deposit -> Select USD

When you are done, click Home -> Deposit -> Fiat tab -> Instant transfer (ACH). Add a bank account

We will Transfer USD from Funding to Trading Account. Enter deposited amount (or click Transfer All)

From Home, go to the trading tab, and select UST/USD pair. Select as much as you want and click Buy UST.

Once the order is filled ( usually it will be instant) go to the Earn section. Select UST -> Continue. Add “some” amount and enjoy the rewards. The flexible rewards means that you can cancel the contract and withdraw your funds at any time!
