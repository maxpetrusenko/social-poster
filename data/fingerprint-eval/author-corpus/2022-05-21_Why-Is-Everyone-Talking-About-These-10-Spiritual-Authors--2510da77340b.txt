## Why Is Everyone Talking About These 10 Spiritual Authors?

### Question your reality

I’ve looked at so many authors and those are in my heart! Essentially they are all talking about the same thing, but you might understand one but not another. If you are just at the beginning of your spiritual journey, you will catch the differences in their words after some time. You will see the dimension from which they are talking.

I’m sure you heard of at least a few names from this list:

Yogananda is a great teacher that will explain how to get to know “God” in a lifetime. His greatest book is “Autobiography of a Yogi” which has brought a lot of attention to his teachings. Self-Realization Fellowship is an organization started by Yogananda that still brings lots of value across the globe.

Krishnamurti became popular pretty early. He didn’t want to form another society/cult/sect but eventually, he created the “Krishnamurti foundation” which is still a form of a society…

Zeland is a pretty private person. There are not as many facts about his life. He is a Russian mystic that had interests in the field of quantum physics, computer technology, and books.

Like all Buddhists, Thich taught people compassion and mindfulness all around the world

Sadhguru is an enlightened yogi and mystic. He is driving his bike all around the world to teach people about the most important things about life.

Come to Blavatsky only if you feel like you know a lot about occult/mysticism/spirituality. She will prove you wrong, and that you have still a lot to learn.

His experiences are unique… I feel like his teachings are — “Everything I wanted to say but didn’t know how”. Some of his words will be a shock to newcomers.

Talks about shamanism and plant medicines. In the beginning, you might think that all of his works are fantasy, not real. But the deeper you go, the better you understand.

Alan had the rare talent to explain unexplainable things. Wrote a lot of beautiful books that are still popular today.

Osho emphasized the importance of meditation, creativity, and sex. Those qualities were suppressed for a long time in people.

With globalization, there are so many spiritual authors it is even hard to count. Every one of them brings a unique point of view. Deepak Chopra, Ram Das, and Mooji are just a few more authors whose works deserve your attention.
