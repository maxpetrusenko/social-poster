## What is Metaverse?

### It’s the future and it’s already here.

Metaverse can be a lot of things.

The hype seems to connect everything to the blockchain. Now, this term could mean something like a decentralized virtual platform based on blockchain technology where you buy land and use crypto as an exchange medium.

But it could be a computer program that simulated a version of the real world (a term coined in a novel called Snow Crash), but where your interactions don’t get reset and are dependent on all your past interactions.

It could be a video game similar to Sims or Second Life.

It could be Facebook, creating a virtual world on top of the real world using augmented and virtual reality. It could be pretty cool, one can imagine seeing beautiful worlds using top-notch graphics. But the Facebook reality’s more likely to be “putting ad banners on your coffee mug” or “selling you a VR car where you need to pay for the gas”.

It could be a VRChat with its full-body avatars. Maybe you would even be able to import your own body using Metahero.

It could be a reality that is portrayed in the movie/book “Ready Player One”.

Or...it can be none of these things. This article, for example, offers a completely different outlook.

> MMO veteran Raph Koster has recently written a series of articles breaking down how game objects work, and he tackles the many difficulties of making even simple items carry between games. “We should not underestimate the magnitude of the task of figuring out what value a cooked pie from Breath of the Wild has in Halo — or mapping every game to every other game,” he says.

> Tech companies are going to keep pushing the metaverse, and eventually we’ll all have to deal with it. But in the meantime, don’t feel like you’ve lost your mind when every pitch for it feels absolutely meaningless. The metaverse is most definitely bullshit.

I think we still don’t know how the Metaverse will look like. And it’s ok. I would encourage people to be more present as we choose our own future. We can go “yoga path” and learn so much about ourselves or we can give up our attention and choose “VR path” creating a virtual world on top of the virtual world without an end.

As for now, Metaverse is a cool word that definitely opens a lot of financial and creative opportunities.
