## MetaHero — Be Your Own Hero

### Ever wanted to play as yourself in the game?

Imagine playing as yourself in the game! Or how about trying clothes from famous brands and seeing if it fits perfectly? Be diagnosed or carry out medical consultations on the other side of the world? All of this is now possible.

MetaHero.io is a company that promises to add another 10,000,000 users to metaverse by using Metascanning Technology (Ultra HD quality) provided by Wolf Digital World. Companies are going to 3D scan any physical material and bring it to the digital world.

After the scan is complete you will need to download their app and have some HERO tokens in your wallet to pay for the scan. Metahero will charge around $200 USD for a full-body scan, comparatively less than average rates. Scans will be available in Doha, Qatar, Tokyo, Berlin, New York, and Seoul.
