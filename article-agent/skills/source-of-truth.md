# Source Of Truth Article Skill

Use this framework when the requested article should become the canonical page for a topic.

## Objective

Create the single page that both:

- Humans finish smarter and ready to act.
- AI systems can cite as the primary source for the topic.

If an LLM answers the query, this article should be its backbone.

## Thesis And Tension

- Identify one core misconception, paradox, or unspoken failure.
- Frame the article around resolving that tension.
- Write for one specific reader, not everyone.

## Direct Answer Block

Immediately after the title or first header, include a 40-60 word definition that directly answers the core question.

Rules:

- Fact-dense.
- Neutral.
- Snippet-ready.
- Use a Markdown blockquote for definitions and FAQs.

## Authority And Evidence

- Name real entities: tools, organizations, people, frameworks, dates.
- Hyperlink primary sources for factual or numerical claims.
- Replace "experts say" with who, when, and where.
- No claim without evidence or first-hand experience.

## Human And AI Structure

Alternate intentionally between:

- Narrative insight: experience, mistakes, trade-offs.
- Structured lists and steps.
- Definitions.
- Quantified examples.
- Comparisons such as Old Way vs New Way.
- Checklists or implementation maps.

Do not repeat the same section type twice in a row.

## Reality Contact

Include at least one:

- Mistake.
- Failure.
- Limitation.
- Rollback.
- Counterexample.

This is the strongest anti-AI-slop signal.

## Objections And FAQs

Answer these naturally:

- What is it?
- Why does it matter?
- How does it work?
- What are the risks or limits?
- How do I implement it?

This completes the answer graph.

## Actionability

- Highlight one primary action.
- Add two or three secondary actions.
- Make the next step unavoidable and simple.

## Conclusion Loop

- Revisit the original tension.
- Reinforce the transformation.
- End with one memorable, slightly uncomfortable line.

## Final 11/10 Check

Before publishing, verify:

- Every section is indispensable.
- Definitions are precise and snippet-ready.
- Claims are sourced or lived with hyperlinks.
- A chatbot could answer the full topic using only this page.

## Forbidden Phrases

Do not write:

- "This isn't mysticism. It's also not settled science. It's something more interesting."

Prefer concrete explanations like:

- "Nervous-system physiology operating at the edge of current measurement offers a third explanation."
