# Write pipeline

read_when: changing the `/generate-article` skill, `scripts/write_pipeline`, or how an article package reaches the review stop.

One `/generate-article` invocation runs 20 stages and stops for Max's review. The agent (Sonnet through `claude -p`) writes prose and judgement artifacts. `scripts/write_pipeline` is the deterministic half: stage state, hash-bound artifacts, cache, validators, our own fingerprint metrics, and calls to the existing evaluator modules. It has no publish command and never mutates Medium.

## Commands

`python -m scripts.write_pipeline <cmd> --package P` with `init`, `status [--json]`, `next`, `begin <stage>`, `submit <stage> --file F [--report R]`, `run brief|review|critic|fpverify|integrity|hash|package|stop`, `antifp baseline|rank|try|finish`, `repair try --file F [--report R]|done`, `fpverify measure|try --file F --signal S|done`, `finalize`, `revalidate`, `rebase --reason`, `block <stage> --reason`.

Exit: 0 ok, 1 artifact rejected, 2 usage or waiting on an upstream stage, 3 NOT_READY, 4 BLOCKED (model or evaluator unavailable, retry later), 5 QUARANTINED, 6 FINAL.md edited after PASS.

## Stages

source, research, angle, outline, brief, draft, validate, editorial, voice, antifp, review, title, images, critic, repair, fpverify, integrity, hash, package, stop.

- `brief` (deterministic, no file) builds a generation brief of 400 words at most from the author-corpus profile (`authorprofile.py`, on `scripts.fingerprint_eval.metrics`), the evaluator's template and transition definitions, the evidence map and the outline: sentence and paragraph percentile targets, lists of three items at most, banned openers, closers and vocabulary, transition and repetition limits, punctuation habits. It records the hierarchy factual > source/link > semantic > quality > voice > fingerprint. `draft`, `editorial` and `voice` must carry `{"brief_sha256": <sha256 of the brief artifact>}` in their `--report` or they are rejected.
- `fpverify` runs after critic and repair and before the integrity gate. It measures the first draft (baseline) and the final candidate with the evaluator metrics (sentence and paragraph regularity, structural, n-gram, list and rhetorical repetition, burstiness, author-corpus distance, templates, em dashes, transitions), each against the author's own percentile band. If significant signals remain, `fpverify try` allows two targeted rounds per run; each is re-measured, must move the targeted signal without worsening the total, and must pass the edit guard (no link lost, no number or claim changed or invented, three blocks at most) and the claims gate with the source notes and evidence ledger. `run fpverify` or `fpverify done` records the stage; remaining signals are reported, not blocking. Integrity then gates the fingerprint-verified bytes.
- Each stage's artifact is stored under `write-pipeline/artifacts/` with a bundle hash. A stage is DONE only while its recorded input hashes (its dependencies, the pipeline version and the framework hash) still match; otherwise it is STALE. Resubmitting identical bytes is a cache hit and invalidates nothing downstream.
- `antifp` measures `scripts.fingerprint_eval.metrics` (template hits, em dashes, repeated n-grams, one-sentence paragraphs, transitions). A try is kept only if the targeted signal and the composite both improve, the edit is local (3 blocks at most), the deterministic guard holds (no link or number lost or invented, headings, code, images and frozen blocks unchanged, no invented experience) and the claims gate (`fingerprint_eval --gate`) passes. No third-party AI detector is called or targeted. A draft still heavy after the loop ends NOT_READY.
- `title` and `images` build the final frame. The candidate and the reference frame share the same title, subtitle and images, so the gate compares only body edits. The reference is the text after the voice pass, before any anti-fingerprint edit, bound through `evals/prepublish-v<N>.json`.
- `critic` is a separate `claude -p` process that sees only the framework, the evidence ledger and the article. A finding counts as major only if its quoted passage is in the article. The budget is three rounds per run, signed in state: resubmitting a title, subtitle, image or caption, or a fingerprint repair, never refills it. With majors open after round three the CLI itself writes NOT_READY. `repair` edits may reword, cut and add sentences. Every added sentence is checked on its own (numbers, links, invented first-person experience, negation, hedge terms; then the evaluator's added-claim support check against the evidence ledger and source notes, bound in by `gaterun`). Deleted material must be declared in the `--report` `removals` (same mechanism as editorial cuts; a link-bearing sentence cannot be cut). A lost link, a changed claim or an unsupported addition is rejected; three rejections end NOT_READY. A stale critic still blocks repair.
- `integrity` writes FINAL.md and runs `release authorize --max-repairs 0` then `release verify`, always, on the exact bytes. It never reuses an earlier PASS. Content failure found by the deterministic precheck is NOT_READY; an evaluator quarantine is QUARANTINED (sticky for the same bytes); an infrastructure failure is BLOCKED. `FINGERPRINT_EVAL_WORKSPACE` points inside the package so the production ACTIVE selector is not replaced. Publishing still needs the normal `release authorize` and `release verify` in the production workspace.
- `package` writes FINAL.html, a Medium review bound to the final bytes, a `publish_route decide` recommendation, and PACKAGE.md with the sections TITLE, SUBTITLE, ARTICLE, SOURCES, IMAGES (provenance, captions, ALT), EDITORIAL SCORECARD, AUTHOR-VOICE RESULT (metric), FINGERPRINT BASELINE, FINGERPRINT FINAL, FINGERPRINT CHANGES, REMAINING FINGERPRINT SIGNALS, CLAIM CHECK, LINK CHECK, SOURCE CHECK, STRUCTURE CHECK, SEMANTIC PRESERVATION, MEDIUM REVIEW (route and topically relevant author-input suggestions), EXACT FINAL HASH, READY/NOT_READY. A run that ends NOT_READY before any final PASS gets PACKAGE.md, FINAL.md and FINAL.html from the CLI too, with the open critic findings and a NOT READY banner. `stop` sets READY_FOR_REVIEW.
- A change to FINAL.md after PASS is detected on the next command (state USER_MODIFIED, exit 6). `revalidate` adopts the edit as the candidate, reruns the critic and the final gate, and repackages. A claim-changing edit fails closed; `rebase --reason` accepts it as the new reference and records an override.

- Upstream rework limit: a finished content stage (source through voice) resubmitted with different bytes after later stages ran counts as a rework; a second one ends the run NOT_READY. Reverting to bytes the stage already had is not a rework. Title and images are not limited.
- Author-input suggestions need topical relevance (term overlap with the title, headings and claims); others are omitted. A provenance that negates a video frame ("no video frames") is not treated as one.

## Trust model and residuals (Codex re-check)

- `state.json` and every stage record carry an HMAC-SHA256 from the evaluator's record key (`scripts/fingerprint_eval/record.py`). An unsigned, edited or malformed state is moved aside as `state.json.invalid-<hash>` and replaced by a persisted NOT_READY state; it is never repaired silently.
- `status`, `stop` and every READY_FOR_REVIEW decision recompute the final verification from the files: FINAL.md hash against the integrity and hash records, the signed release authorization and gate record (PASS, same bytes), `release verify`, a strictly validated `route.json` bound to that hash, `publish_route verify`, and the hashes of FINAL.md, FINAL.html (re-rendered), PACKAGE.md and route.json recorded in the package stage. Any mismatch is NOT_READY.
- The gate child (`fingerprint_eval run` and `release`) receives `LLM_GATEWAY_API_KEY` and `LLM_GATEWAY_URL`; no other child does. `claude -p` children keep the subscription-only env.
- Input files (`submit --file/--report`, `antifp try --file`, `repair try --file`) and the framework path must resolve (symlinks followed) inside the package, an exact configured framework file (`WRITE_PIPELINE_FRAMEWORK` or the default), or a root listed in `WRITE_PIPELINE_INPUT_ROOTS` (os.pathsep separated).
- Residual, accepted: same-user risk. A process running as the owner can read the record key and forge a signed state; the HMAC stops edits by anything that cannot.
- Residual, accepted (review item 10): model aliases such as `claude:sonnet` are not bound to a runtime version in the evaluator cache key, and the embedding runtime digest is best effort. Pinning aliases to a resolved model version is deferred.
- Editorial and voice stages may declare `removals` in the report. A declared sentence that exactly matches a reference sentence (inline markup and whitespace ignored) and is really gone from the candidate is an intentional cut: it is taken out of that stage's claims-gate reference, recorded in the state and in the HMAC-signed `write-pipeline/removals-ledger.json`. Changed and added claims still block, and the final integrity gate is unchanged (it compares the post-editorial reference with the final bytes).
- The validate stage's unresolved-claims semantic check builds its reference from the candidate's own frozen skeleton plus one claims paragraph, so the gate judges claims, not structure. A FAIL with any non-claims category is inconclusive and ends the stage NOT_READY; it never counts as "unresolved claims absent".
