## Variant: Annotation Handoff Studio

### Design stance
A cleaner productized workflow for turning visual annotations into a Codex-ready iteration brief.

### Key choices
- Layout: left process rail, center browser-frame mockup, right annotation/scratchpad/diagram panel.
- Typography: modern sans-serif to feel like a usable design-tool feature rather than a pure sketch.
- Color: white cards, soft blue/yellow accents, warm off-white background; no dark mode.
- Interaction: click pins or notes to sync focus, generate a Codex brief, toggle Mermaid-style state diagram, toggle primary action state.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: directly answering “How do you annotate your mockups and feed them back to Codex?”
- Weak at: less visibly rough than Parker’s Balsamiq recommendation, so it may invite premature polish.

### Best for
- Teams that want repeatable annotation/handoff mechanics embedded into Social Poster or an internal design-prep workspace.
